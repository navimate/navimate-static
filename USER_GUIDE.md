## 2. How to Acquire Walk Credits & How the Credit Firewall Works

NaviMate never locks you into a recurring monthly subscription. Instead, it uses **Pay-As-You-Go Walk Credits** that **never expire**, paired with a strict **Zero-Cost Firewall** so free actions never touch your balance.

### 2.1 Four Ways to Open the Walk Credits Store
You can top up Walk Credits at any time from four places:
1. **Top-Left Toolbar Badge (Any Tab):** Tap the green **`✨ N Credits`** (or orange **`🔒 0 Credits • Demo`**) pill in the top-left corner of the **Tour**, **Explore**, or **History** tabs.
2. **Settings Tab:** Open **Settings** → **Credits & Access Mode** (the first section at the top) → tap **`Top Up Walk Credits (From $2.99)`**.
3. **Tour Setup Screen (When at 0 Credits):** On **Tour** → **Plan New Tour**, scroll to the bottom and tap the green **`Get Walk Credits (From $2.99)`** button.
4. **Explore Tab (When at 0 Credits):** Open **Explore** and tap the green **`Get Walk Credits (From $2.99)`** button.

## 3. How to Minimize or Maximize Credit Consumption

NaviMate gives you explicit controls so you can dial credit usage down to near-zero on a budget trip—or dial storytelling depth up to maximum when exploring a bucket-list city.

### 3.1 How to Minimize Credit Consumption (The Budget / Low-Burn Playbook)

Follow these 7 steps to stretch a single `$2.99` (`100-credit`) pack across an entire vacation:

1. **Keep Guide Response Depth on `Short-Form (2 cr)`:**
   - **Where:** On the **Active Route** mic bar, near the top of the **Explore** screen (`Depth: Short (2 cr)`), or in **Settings → Guide Response Depth**.
   - **Why it saves credits:** Cuts the cost of every Explore discovery, Live Voice Q&A answer, and Camera Lens photo in half (`2 credits` instead of `4 credits`) while delivering punchy 2–3 sentence (~20-second) stories.
2. **Set an `Explore Landmark Ceiling` (or Set It to `0 — Q&A Only`):**
   - **Where:** **Settings → Guide Response Depth, Landmark Cap & Non-Repetition → Explore Landmark Ceiling**.
   - **Why it saves credits:**
     - Setting it to **`3 Landmarks`** caps automatic GPS proximity stories at a maximum of `6 credits` (`Short-Form`) per Explore walk.
     - Setting it to **`0 (Q&A Only)`** stops Explore from ever triggering automatic stories as you walk past monuments (`0 automatic credits`), while keeping the live radar map and microphone active so you only pay `2 credits` when *you* choose to ask a question or snap a photo.
3. **Keep `Prevent Repeat Landmarks & Stops` Turned `ON` (`SAVES CREDITS`):**
   - **Where:** **Settings → Guide Response Depth, Landmark Cap & Non-Repetition → Prevent Repeat Landmarks & Stops** (enabled by default).
   - **Why it saves credits:** NaviMate remembers every stop you have visited on a Planned Tour, every landmark discovered in Explore, and every building photographed with Camera Lens. When this toggle is `ON`, NaviMate automatically excludes those places when planning new tours or walking in Explore in the same city—so you **never pay twice for the same landmark**.
4. **Mute the Mic or Disable `Voice Interruption & Ask Guide (Mic)` When Chatting with Friends:**
   - **Where:** Tap the **`Mute`** button on the right side of the `WalkingMicStatusBar` during a walk, **or** tap the top-left **`Credits`** pill → **Active Feature Matrix** → toggle **`Voice Interruption & Ask Guide (Mic)`** to **`OFF`**.
   - **Why it saves credits:** If you are walking and talking with travel companions, muting the mic or turning off the Voice Mic feature guarantees that side conversations are never mistaken for questions to the guide.
5. **Turn `Require trigger phrase on headphones` `ON` in Settings:**
   - **Where:** **Settings → Interrupting the Guide → Require trigger phrase on headphones** (`ON` by default).
   - **Why it saves credits:** While the guide is speaking, it will only stop and listen for a paid Q&A turn if you explicitly say your trigger phrase (*"Excuse me"*, *"Hey Guide"*, *"Stop"*, or *"Wait"*).
6. **Preview Routes Free & Trim Extra Stops Before Generating Audio:**
   - **Where:** **Tour → Plan New Tour → `Start Planning AI Tour`**.
   - **Why it saves credits:** Stage 1 (`Review Route`) costs **0 credits**. If a 90-minute draft proposes 6 stops (`36 credits`), you can swipe left on 1 or 2 stops in the **Review Route** list to trim it to 4 stops (`24 credits`) before tapping **Confirm & Generate Audio**.
7. **Use `0-Credit` Local Voice Commands & Replay Saved Tours:**
   - Short commands like *"pause"*, *"resume"*, *"next stop"*, *"previous stop"*, *"repeat that"*, *"where am I"*, and *"open maps"* are processed on-device for **0 credits**.
   - Every generated tour is saved with its audio in **History → Saved Tours**. Load and re-walk it (or share it with a partner) as many times as you want for **0 credits**.

### 3.2 How to Maximize Storytelling Depth & Discovery (The Historian Playbook)

When you want the richest possible cultural, architectural, and historical experience:

1. **Switch Guide Response Depth to `Long-Form (4 cr)`:**
   - **Where:** Tap **`Long (4 cr)`** on the **Explore** screen, on the **Active Route** mic bar, or in **Settings → Guide Response Depth**.
   - **What it unlocks:** Expands every Explore discovery, voice Q&A reply, and Camera Lens identification to 4–6 richly detailed sentences (~45–55 seconds, 160–220 words) with deeper historical dates, anecdotes, and architectural context.
2. **Set `Explore Landmark Ceiling` to `15 Landmarks` or `Unlimited`:**
   - **Where:** **Settings → Explore Landmark Ceiling**.
   - **What it unlocks:** Allows the guide to narrate every notable square, church, palace, and hidden courtyard you pass during a long afternoon wander without stopping at 5 landmarks.
3. **Turn On `Let the guide suggest detours`:**
   - **Where:** **Settings → Detours → Let the guide suggest detours** (`ON`).
   - **What it unlocks:** While walking between planned tour stops, NaviMate quietly checks for standout hidden gems just off your path (within `600m` extra walk) and asks if you'd like to splice a detour into your route.
4. **Combine a `Guide Character` with `Time Travel`:**
   - **Where:** **Settings → Your Guide's Character** (`The Architect`, `The Local Gossip`, `The Ghost Hunter`, `Family & Kids`) + **Settings → Time Travel** (`Before 1500`, `1500–1800`, `The 1800s`, `The 1900s`).
   - **What it unlocks:** Walking the same street twice with different settings yields a completely new tour—for example, walking Rome with **Before 1500** + **The Architect** vs. **1500–1800** + **The Local Gossip**. (Remember to toggle **Prevent Repeat Landmarks & Stops** `OFF` if you want to revisit the exact same monuments under a new persona or century!)
5. **Take 3+ Photos with `Snap & Ask` (`What am I looking at?`) During Your Walk:**
   - **Where:** Tap **`What am I looking at?`** on the **Active Route** or **Explore** screen.
   - **What it unlocks:** Not only does the guide tell you the story behind the facade, statue, or plaque in front of you, but your own photographs are automatically featured in your **Souvenir Walk Postcard** and **9:16 Video Recap Reel** at the end of the walk.

3. **Resume the Interrupted Story (`0 Credits`):**
   - If you interrupted a landmark story early, your guide finishes answering your question and politely asks: *"Would you like me to carry on with `<Landmark Name>`?"*
   - Simply say **`"Yes"`** (or **`"Carry on"`**) to replay/finish the landmark story at **`0 credits`**, or say **`"No"`** to move on.
4. **Use `0-Credit` Local Voice Reflex Commands:**

     - **`"Previous stop"`** / **`"Go back"`** → Jumps back to the previous tour stop.
     - **`"Repeat that"`** / **`"Say that again"`** → Replays the current landmark story at `0 credits`.

   - *(Tip: If you don't see it, tap the top-left **`Credits`** pill → **Active Feature Matrix** and make sure **`Camera Landmark Lens ("What is this?")`** is toggled **`ON`**.)*
2. **Capture the Landmark or Plaque (`2 cr` Short / `4 cr` Long):**
   - Tap **`What am I looking at?`**. NaviMate automatically silences any current narration and pauses the microphone so the camera shutter never triggers a false voice question.
   - Frame the building, monument, statue, or historical plaque in the camera viewfinder and tap the shutter button, then tap **`Use Photo`**.
3. **Listen to the Multimodal Visual Identification:**
   - NaviMate combines `gemini-3.8-flash` Vision with your exact GPS coordinates and all Wikimedia landmarks within `250m` to disambiguate lookalike buildings on the same street (or reads plaques aloud if **Eyes-Free Accessibility Mode** is enabled).
   - Your guide speaks the story immediately, pins a camera icon (`📷`) onto your map, records the landmark in your **Visited Landmarks** memory, and saves your high-resolution photo on-device in `LensPhotoStore` for your post-walk Postcard and Reel.

### Journey 7: Replaying Saved Tours Offline, Creating QR Walk Postcards & Exporting 9:16 Video Reels

**Goal:** Replay a downloaded tour in Airplane Mode for `0 credits`, write notes in your Walk Journal, and share a Souvenir Walk Postcard or 9:16 Instagram/TikTok Video Reel.

1. **Replay Any Saved Tour Offline at `0 Credits`:**
   - Open **Tab 3 (`History`)** and make sure **`Saved Tours`** is selected in the top segmented control.
   - Find any tour card (look for the purple **`〰️ Gemini AI Voices Saved`** badge) and tap the green **`Load Tour`** button.
   - NaviMate switches straight to **Tab 1 (`Active Route`)**—tap **`START GUIDED TOUR`** to walk and listen to the entire tour offline for **`0 credits`**.
2. **Open Your Walk Journal & Add Personal Notes:**
   - On **Tab 3 (`History`)**, switch the top segmented control to **`Walk Journal`**.
   - Review your lifetime summary banner at the top (**`km walked`**, **`stops seen`**, **`cities`**) and tap any completed walk row.
   - Scroll down to **`Your notes`** and type your favorite memories, café names, or reflections—your notes auto-save as you type.
3. **Create & Share a Souvenir Walk Postcard (`0 Credits`):**
   - Either tap **`Complete ✓`** on the final stop of an active tour, tap the purple **`Postcard`** button on any Saved Tour card, or tap **`Make a postcard`** inside any Walk Journal entry.
   - The **Walk Postcard Sheet** renders a high-resolution `540×675` card featuring:
     - Your route map and stop pins.
     - Your own **Camera Lens (`Snap & Ask`) photos** from the walk (automatically backfilled with `CC BY-SA`-attributed Wikimedia Commons landmark photos if you took fewer than 3 photos).
     - An AI-crafted summary line and walk stats (`distance`, `duration`, `stops`).
     - A scannable **`navimate://walk` QR code** that lets friends import your exact walking route.
   - Tap **`Share Postcard`** to send the image via iMessage, WhatsApp, Instagram, or AirDrop, or save it to your Photos library.
4. **Export a 9:16 Illustrated Walk Recap Video Reel (`1080×1920 MP4`, `0 Credits`):**
   - Inside the same **Walk Postcard Sheet**, tap **`Export 9:16 Video Reel`**.
   - NaviMate renders a smooth `1080×1920` vertical H.264 MP4 video animating your route map, stop photographs, titles, and Wikimedia attributions—ready to post directly to Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts.

### Journey 8: Customizing Voices, Personas, Eras, Eyes-Free Accessibility & Credit Controls

**Goal:** Tailor your guide's voice, regional accent, character persona, historical time-travel era, accessibility cues, and credit guardrails in **Tab 4 (`Settings`)**.

1. **Pick & Preview Your Guide Voice and Accent:**
   - Open **Tab 4 (`Settings`)** and scroll to **`Guide Voice`**.
   - Tap **`Guide`** to choose from **30 Gemini Studio Voices** (e.g., *Aoede — Breezy*, *Charon — Informative*, *Fenrir — Excitable*).
   - Tap **`Accent`** to select a regional inflection.
   - Tap **`Preview <Voice>`** to hear a live sample in your chosen **Guide Language**.
   - *(Optional)* Tap **`Between stops`** if you want a second, contrasting voice to narrate the city history between stops.
2. **Choose Your Guide's Character (`GuidePersona`):**
   - In **`Your Guide's Character`**, open the **`Character`** picker and choose **`Classic Guide`**, **`The Architect`**, **`The Local Gossip`**, **`The Ghost Hunter`**, or **`Family & Kids`**.
   - If a **`✨ Use <Voice> — the voice that suits <Character>`** button appears beneath the picker, tap it to adopt the recommended voice pairing.
3. **Activate Historical Time Travel (`HistoricalEra`):**
   - In **`Time Travel`**, open **`Narrate from`** and select **`Before 1500`**, **`1500–1800`**, **`The 1800s`**, or **`The 1900s`** (or **`Today`** to return to the present).
   - On your next stop or Explore discovery, your guide will describe what stood on that exact ground during your chosen century!
4. **Configure Eyes-Free Accessibility, Spatial Audio & Haptics:**
   - Toggle **`Directional compass chime before stories`** (`ON` by default) for 3D stereo-panned landmark chimes.
   - Toggle **`Hands-Free Voice Directions`** and **`Haptic Turn Cues`** (`ON` by default) for pocket navigation.
   - Toggle **`Eyes-Free Accessibility Mode`** **`ON`** if you want clock-face + step-count orientation cues, tactile/acoustic descriptions, step-free routing preference, and the Sign & Plaque Reader.
5. **Inspect or Edit What Your Guide Remembers About You:**
   - Scroll to the bottom of **Settings** and tap **`What My Guide Knows About Me`**.
   - Review your learned **Favorite Topics**, **Avoided Topics**, **Cross-City Memories**, and **Active Story Arcs**. Swipe left on any item to remove it, or tap **`Clear All Guide Memory`** to start fresh.

## 6. Complete Voice Command & Siri Cheat Sheet

### 6.1 In-App Local Voice Reflex Commands (`0 Credits`, Instant On-Device Execution)
Speak any of these phrases while a **Guided Tour** or **Explore** walk is listening:

| What to Say | What NaviMate Does (`0 Credits`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Pause"** / **"Pause tour"** / **"Hold on"** | Pauses narration, microphone listening, and proximity alerts in place. |
| **"Resume"** / **"Continue"** / **"Carry on"** | Resumes a paused walk—or accepts the guide's offer to finish an interrupted landmark story. |
| **"Next stop"** / **"Skip"** / **"Next landmark"** | Jumps immediately to the next stop on your Guided Tour. |
| **"Previous stop"** / **"Go back"** | Jumps back to the previous stop on your Guided Tour. |
| **"Repeat that"** / **"Say that again"** | Replays the current landmark story from the beginning at `0 credits`. |
| **"Where am I?"** | Speaks your current street/locality and distance to the active stop. |
| **"Open maps"** / **"Directions in maps"** | Opens your walking route in Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android). |
| **"Yes"** / **"Add to route"** *(when a detour is offered)* | Accepts a pending detour offer (`6 credits` to generate the detour stop). |
| **"No"** / **"No thanks"** *(when a detour or story resume is offered)* | Declines the detour or resume offer (`0 credits`). |
