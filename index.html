First-Launch Privacy Consent & Zero-Cost Bundled Demo Walk ("Couch-to-Walk")

In four quick onboarding slides, pick your language (1 of 15) and tap Start Free Demo Tour to launch any of the 5 bundled city walks (Firenze, London, Paris, Rome, NYC) immediately at 0 credits.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  3. Start Listening (0 Credits): NaviMate opens Tour → Active Route. Tap START GUIDED TOUR (or Play Story on the bottom bar) to hear pre-recorded studio narration offline. Tap Change City in the top demo banner anytime to try the other 4 free cities.

Journey 2 • Custom AI Tour Planning

Free Route Draft Preview, Interactive Stop Reordering & 100% Offline Audio Prefetch

Choose a 30–180 minute loop (or a Point-to-Point commute to your destination), pick a theme (General History, Hidden Gems & Secrets, Architecture & Design, Local Food & Culture, or a custom topic), and tap Start Planning AI Tour for 0 credits. Review and reorder stops on the Draft sheet, then tap Confirm & Generate Audio (6 credits/stop) to download 100% of the tour audio for offline walking.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Set Your Starting Point & Loop vs. Point-to-Point: On Tour → Plan New Tour, keep Use My Current Location ON (or turn it OFF and type any city/hotel in Or type city/address). Keep Finish Tour Where I Started ON for a circular loop, or toggle it OFF to enter a Point-to-Point Destination.
  2. Choose Duration, Theme, Language & Must-See Stops: Pick a Target Duration (30m–180m), select a Tour Theme card (or type a custom request in Or describe what you'd like to see…), choose your Guide Language, and optionally list Must-See Places.

  4. Confirm & Download Offline Audio (6 Credits / Stop): Tap Confirm & Generate Audio to write all stop stories and pre-download 100% of the narration to your phone (🎧 Audio ready offline).

Journey 3 • Active Guided Tour

Street-by-Street Turn Navigation, 14-Block Stop Cards, Historical Eras & Detours

During a guided walk, NaviMate announces street-by-street turns. Arriving within 45m of a stop automatically folds the directions card and opens the segmented Story, Fun fact (Insider Tips), and Next walk (Nearby) tabs. Use the split Pause/Resume and Stop controls anytime without losing your progress, switch Historical Eras on the fly, or accept a Proactive Detour when a hidden gem is spotted nearby.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Start Your Walk: On Tour → Active Route, tap START GUIDED TOUR. Follow the expanded Walking directions card (or tap Open Route in Apple / Google Maps) and pocket haptic turn cues.
  2. Arrive Within 45m & Explore Stop Tabs: Stepping within 45m of the stop shows ✓ You're here, folds the directions card, and starts the story. Switch between the Story, Fun fact, and Next walk tabs anytime.
  3. Pause / Resume or Add a Detour: Tap PAUSE (right half of top button) for a coffee break and RESUME to continue mid-sentence. Tap Anything interesting nearby? (0 cr to check) on the purple Detour Card and tap Add to route (6 cr) to splice a hidden gem into your walk.
  4. Navigate Stops & Finish: Use the pinned bottom bar (<, Play Story / Stop Audio, Next >), and tap Complete ✓ on the final stop to log your walk and open your Souvenir Walk Postcard.

Journey 4 • Spontaneous Explore Mode

Directional Cone Radar, Spatial Audio Compass Ping & Live Solar/Weather Context

Want to wander without a fixed route? Open Explore, select Short-Form (2 cr) or Long-Form (4 cr), and tap Start Exploring. Using true-north heading and AirPods head-tracking (GAZE), NaviMate scans a 100m forward cone, plays a 3D spatial chime from the landmark's direction, and weaves live solar light and weather into the narration.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Pick Your Depth & Session Cap: Open Tab 2 (Explore) and select Short (2 cr) (~20s) or Long (4 cr) (~50s) on the Depth card directly below the top button. Optionally set your Explore Landmark Ceiling in Settings (0, 3, 5, 10, 15, or Unlimited).
  2. Tap START EXPLORING: Both the 75m–100m forward walking cone and continuous hands-free voice listening activate immediately.
  3. Follow the 3D Spatial Chime & Gaze Radar: With AirPods Pro/Max, the radar tracks your head direction (GAZE • <deg>° • 500M MAP); otherwise it uses your phone's compass (FORWARD SCAN). Turn toward the 3D chime to spot each landmark!
  4. Pause or End Session: Tap PAUSE / RESUME anytime, or tap END SESSION when finished to save the walk to your Walk Journal.

Journey 5 • Hands-Free Voice Q&A

Full-Duplex Voice Interruption, Live Grounded Answers & 0-Credit Reflex Commands

Speak naturally while wearing headphones—or say "Hey Guide" / "Excuse me" on speaker—to interrupt narration mid-sentence. NaviMate halts playback immediately, answers your question using grounded Wikipedia and live weather data over Gemini Live, and asks if you'd like to resume the interrupted landmark story.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Interrupt Mid-Sentence (Barge-In): Say your trigger phrase ("Excuse me", "Hey Guide", "Stop", or "Wait"—or speak directly on headphones if trigger phrase is toggled OFF in Settings) followed by your question (2 cr Short / 4 cr Long).
  2. Resume Interrupted Stories Free (0 Credits): After answering your question, the guide asks: "Would you like me to carry on with <Landmark>?" Say "Yes" or "Carry on" to finish the story at 0 credits.
  3. Use 0-Credit Voice Reflex Commands: Say "pause", "resume", "next stop", "previous stop", "repeat that", "where am I", or "open maps" for instant on-device control at 0 credits. Tap Mute on the mic bar anytime when chatting with friends.

Journey 6 • Camera Landmark Lens

Snap & Ask Multimodal Visual Identification on Both Tour & Explore

Spot an intriguing facade, statue, or plaque? Tap the camera icon (Snap & Ask) during any Tour or Explore session. Combining Gemini Vision with a 250m Wikimedia candidate radius, NaviMate identifies the exact building, speaks its story, and saves your photo for your Walk Postcard and 9:16 Recap Reel.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Tap "What am I looking at?": Located directly below the microphone status bar on both Active Route and Explore (automatically pauses narration and mic listening while the camera is open).
  2. Snap the Photo (2 cr Short / 4 cr Long): Frame any building, statue, monument, or historical plaque and tap the shutter button.
  3. Listen & Save for Your Postcard: Your guide identifies the exact landmark using GPS + Wikimedia candidates within 250m (or reads plaques aloud in Eyes-Free Accessibility Mode) and saves your photo for your Souvenir Walk Postcard and 9:16 Video Reel.

Budget / Low-Burn Playbook

How to Minimize Credit Consumption

  1. Keep Depth on Short-Form (2 cr): Cuts every Explore story, voice Q&A reply, and Camera Lens photo cost in half (2 cr instead of 4 cr).
  2. Set an Explore Landmark Ceiling: In Settings, pick 3 Landmarks (max 6 cr auto-stories per walk) or 0 (Q&A Only) (0 cr auto-stories; pay only when you ask a question or snap a photo).
  3. Keep "Prevent Repeat Landmarks & Stops" ON: Automatically skips landmarks you've already visited across Tour, Explore, and Camera Lens so you never pay twice for the same place.
  4. Mute Mic or Use Trigger Phrases When Chatting: Keep Require trigger phrase on headphones ON, tap Mute on the mic bar, or toggle Voice Interruption & Ask Guide (Mic) OFF in the top-left Credits & Access sheet when talking with companions.
  5. Preview Routes Free & Replay Saved Tours: Swipe left on extra stops in the 0-credit Review Route sheet before generating audio, use 0-credit voice commands ("pause", "next stop", "repeat that"), and replay downloaded tours from History → Saved Tours for 0 credits.
Historian Deep-Dive Playbook

How to Maximize Storytelling Depth & Discovery

  1. Switch Depth to Long-Form (4 cr): Expands every Explore discovery, voice Q&A answer, and Camera Lens story to 4–6 richly detailed sentences (~45–55 seconds).
  2. Set Explore Landmark Ceiling to 15 or Unlimited: Let the guide narrate every notable square, palace, church, and hidden courtyard on a long afternoon wander.
  3. Enable Proactive Detours: Toggle Let the guide suggest detours ON in Settings → Detours to discover standout hidden gems within 600m of your route.
  4. Combine a Guide Character with Time Travel: Pair The Architect, The Local Gossip, or The Ghost Hunter with Before 1500, 1500–1800, The 1800s, or The 1900s.
  5. Snap 3+ Landmarks with Camera Lens: Tap What am I looking at? during your walk so your own photos headline your Souvenir Walk Postcard and 9:16 Video Recap Reel.
